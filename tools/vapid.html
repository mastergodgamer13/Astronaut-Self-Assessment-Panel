Reminder keys

Makes the two keys the reminder worker needs. Everything happens in this page, in your browser: nothing is sent anywhere.

1. Public key (Cloudflare variable VAPID_PUBLIC, and the "Public key" box in the app)

2. Private key (Cloudflare secret VAPID_PRIVATE_JWK)

Keep the private key secret. Paste it only into Cloudflare. Do not put it in the repository, in a message, or in the app. If it leaks, make new keys and subscribe your devices again.
